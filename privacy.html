Privacy Policy

PureWash business app · Last updated October 29, 2026

The PureWash app is an internal business tool used by PureWash, a window cleaning company, and its staff to manage customers, scheduling, quotes, invoices and payments. This policy explains what the app stores and how it connects to QuickBooks Online and other services.

What the app stores

  • Customer contact details, service addresses, job history, photos, quotes, invoices and payment records that PureWash staff enter.
  • Messages and voicemails exchanged with customers through PureWash's phone and email providers.
  • Staff login details (email and an encrypted password).

QuickBooks Online

When PureWash connects its own QuickBooks Online company, the app uses Intuit's authorization to create and update customers, products/services, invoices and payments in that company, and to read payments recorded there so both systems agree. Access tokens are stored on the server only and are never shown in the browser. PureWash can disconnect at any time from the app's settings or from QuickBooks, which revokes access.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to run PureWash's business: scheduling and performing work, billing, and communicating with customers. It is not sold or used for advertising. It is shared only with the service providers that run the app (database hosting, web hosting, email, phone/text and QuickBooks) and only as needed for those services.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, stored with access controls by staff role, and service keys are kept on the server.

Your choices

Customers can ask PureWash to see, correct or delete their information by contacting PureWash at purewash.net.
